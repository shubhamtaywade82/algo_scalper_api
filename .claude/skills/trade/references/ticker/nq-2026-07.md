# NQ Overnight Session Read (2026-07-21/22)

Live application of [`../overnight-futures-framework.md`](../overnight-futures-framework.md), run twice (21:25 ET and 00:41 ET) — the second pass is the point: overnight drivers decay and must be re-read mid-session.

---

## Setup (21:25 ET)

**Tape**: NQ 29,248, −0.26% vs prior settle 29,316 — a drift, not a gap, after a +1.9% NDX cash day (chip-rebound rally; MU +12%, VIX crushed −1.6 to 17.05).

**Three-complex read**:

| Complex | Print | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Equity futures | NQ −0.26%, ES −0.11% | mild red |
| Safe-haven/inflation | Gold +1.1% ($4,121), Silver +1.5%, WTI +1.1% ($85.3), DXY +0.19 | **bid all night** |
| Asia cash (live) | KOSPI **+5.9%** (verified vs daily history — ±5% swings were the July regime), Nikkei +1.6%, HSI −0.8% | ripping ex-China |

Pattern match: *NQ down + Asia up + safe havens up* → the drag was the **energy/inflation leg** (US–Iran war, 11th night of strikes announced 20:26 ET — a fresh in-session headline), not risk appetite. Asia chip euphoria was the floor, not the driver of the red print.

**Catalyst clock**: GOOGL + TSLA + IBM 7/22 amc; SK Hynix "7/22" in KST = **inside the US night** (est EPS ₩68,565, rev +62% YoY, actuals still null → open two-way gap risk); ECB + Japan CPI Thursday; FOMC the following week → blackout, hawkish "rate-hike-chances" narrative running unopposed.

**Scenarios (issued)**: Base 55% range 29,150–29,350 waiting for prints; Bull 25% Hynix blowout → >29,400; Bear 20% Mideast escalation / oil >$87 → <29,000.

## Second read (00:41 ET) — driver decay

- NQ 29,222 (−0.32%): 25 pts lower, same regime — base scenario tracking.
- **Asia floor thinning**: KOSPI +5.9% → **+4.3%**, Nikkei +1.6% → +1.2% — the pre-print front-running was being taken off, which *explained* the NQ drift without any new bearish headline.
- **Safe-haven grind intact and strengthening**: gold +1.4% ($4,133, night high), WTI +1.23%; Trump "will respond if Houthis escalate" 22:15 ET. The war/inflation leg stayed the active drag.
- Hynix still un-dropped at Korea midday → repriced as a late-night (2–3 AM ET) event; gap risk still open.

## Lessons

1. **The divergence pattern is the attribution.** A bare "NQ −0.3%" supported three different narratives; the three-complex table collapsed it to one (energy/inflation drag) in a single step.
2. **Overnight drivers decay — re-read mid-session.** The 21:25 "Asia +5.9% floor" was +4.3% by 00:41. A single evening read published as "the night's story" would have been stale in three hours.
3. **Convert foreign catalyst dates before saying "nothing tonight."** "SK Hynix 7/22" looked like tomorrow; in KST it was tonight — and its front-running (KOSPI) and unwind were *both* live NQ drivers.
4. **Verify anomalous prints against history before publishing** — KOSPI +5.9% was real (July 2026 regime had daily ±5% swings), but only the daily-history check made it usable.
5. **In-session headlines timestamp the tape**: the 20:26 ET "new round of strikes" print mapped exactly onto the oil/gold leg — attribution with a number *and* a headline, per the framework's discipline.
