# SMC Concepts Knowledge Base

Institutional orderflow is represented by the repeatable cycle of liquidity and structural shifts.

## The institutional Cycle
1. **Liquidity Creation**: Equal Highs/Lows, Session Highs/Lows.
2. **Liquidity Sweep**: Price spikes to capture resting stops.
3. **Displacement**: Large, high-volume candle indicating institutional entry.
4. **Imbalance Creation**: Fair Value Gaps (FVG) left behind.
5. **Rebalancing/Mitigation**: Price returns to fill the FVG or mitigate the Order Block.
6. **Continuation**: Price resumes in the direction of the displacement.

## Core Components
- **BOS (Break of Structure)**: Confirms trend continuation at structural levels.
- **CHOCH (Change of Character)**: First signal of potential trend reversal.
- **FVG (Fair Value Gap)**: Price inefficiency where high/low wicks do not overlap.
- **Order Block (OB)**: The last counter-trend candle before displacement.
- **Liquidity Sweep**: Purposeful movement to trigger stops before the real move.
- **Inducement**: False structure built to trap retail traders.
- **Breaker Block**: A failed OB that becomes a flip zone (S/R flip).
