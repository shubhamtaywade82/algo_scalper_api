# Feature and Indicator Categories

Indicators are structured into the following categories to simplify search and correlation tasks.

## 1. Trend Features
* **EMA/SMA**: Trend direction and alignment slopes.
* **Supertrend**: Volatility-adjusted breakout lines.
* **ADX**: Trend strength metrics (ADX > 25 indicates strong trend).
* **Donchian Channels**: Multi-period highs and lows.

## 2. Momentum Features
* **RSI**: Overbought/oversold boundaries and divergence checks.
* **MACD**: Moving average crossings and histogram velocity.
* **ROC (Rate of Change)**: Momentum speed.

## 3. Volatility Features
* **ATR**: Volatility bands.
* **ATR Percentile**: Current ATR compared to its 100-candle range.
* **Bollinger Bands**: Range expansion and compression boundaries.
* **Historical Volatility**: Standard deviation of log returns.

## 4. Volume Features
* **VWAP**: Intraday volume-weighted average price pivots.
* **Relative Volume (RVOL)**: Spikes in volume compared to normal.
* **Volume Profile**: High-volume node (HVN) levels.

## 5. Option Chain Features
* **Open Interest (OI)**: Net contract participation.
* **OI Velocity (15m change)**: Immediate buildup trends.
* **Put-Call Ratio (PCR)**: Volume and OI sentiments.
* **Greeks**: Delta, Gamma, Theta, and Vega profiles.

## 6. Structure Features
* **BOS / CHOCH**: Structural breakouts and reversal markers.
* **Swing Quality**: Distance between swing highs and lows.
* **Liquidity Sweeps**: Pin-bar reversals below swing zones.
