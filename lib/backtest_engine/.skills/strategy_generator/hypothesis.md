# Hypothesis

Every strategy begins here.

## Examples

- Momentum persists after opening range breakout.
- Strong IV expansion precedes directional movement.
- VWAP rejection creates continuation.
- EMA alignment improves trend probability.
- Volume breakout increases breakout success.
- OI shift predicts trend.
- PCR extremes predict reversals.
- ATR contraction leads to expansion.
- ADX above threshold indicates trend.
- Supertrend works only during expansion.

Never generate entries before defining the hypothesis.
