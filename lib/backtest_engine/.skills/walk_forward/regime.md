# Forward Regime Consistency Analysis

Ensure strategy performance remains consistent across shifting market regimes.

## Regime Audits
* **Regime Shifts**: Track when a forward test window encounters a regime change (e.g. transitioning from low-volatility range to high-volatility trend).
* **Regime win rate**: Audit if out-of-sample win rates fall below target limits in range-bound environments.
* **Expiry cycle stability**: Verify that the walk-forward results hold up during weekly and monthly expiry days.
