# Correlation

Prevent correlated positions.

## Supported Indices

- NIFTY
- BANKNIFTY
- FINNIFTY
- MIDCPNIFTY
- SENSEX
- BANKEX

## Methods

- Cross Correlation
- Rolling Correlation
- Dynamic Correlation
- Regime Correlation

## Rules

- Avoid simultaneous highly correlated bullish positions.
- Limit aggregate directional exposure.
- Control concentration by underlying and expiry.
