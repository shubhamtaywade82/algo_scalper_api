# Regime Dependency Analysis

Examine strategy performance under different market regimes to find edge boundaries.

## Regime Evaluations

1. **Trending vs. Range-Bound**:
   - Compare win rates, average win, and expectancy between trending and range-bound regimes.
   - Flag if the strategy loses all its trending profits during range chop.

2. **High IV vs. Low IV**:
   - Evaluate if the strategy out-performs in high-volatility expansions or low-volatility drifts.

3. **Expiry Day Effects**:
   - Segregate trades executed on expiry days. Option buying often exhibits extreme positive skewness on expiry days.
