# Commissions & Transaction Costs Model

Deduct realistic costs on every trade to calculate true net returns.

## Indian Brokerage & Tax Ecosystem

1. **Brokerage**:
   - Flat 20 Rupees per executed order (buy/sell).

2. **Securities Transaction Tax (STT)**:
   - Charged at `0.125%` on the option premium value upon sale/exit.

3. **Exchange Transaction Charges**:
   - Charged by NSE/BSE (approx. `0.05%` of premium value).

4. **SEBI Turnover Fees**:
   - Flat rate per crore of turnover.

5. **Stamp Duty**:
   - Charged at `0.003%` on option premium value upon purchase/entry.

6. **GST**:
   - `18%` on the sum of Brokerage, Exchange Charges, and SEBI Fees.
