# Market Structure Pattern Playbook

Use these patterns to identify trend phases and market state transitions.

## 1. Break of Structure (BOS)
* **Bullish BOS**: Price closes above the previous swing high. Confirms continuation of the bullish trend.
* **Bearish BOS**: Price closes below the previous swing low. Confirms continuation of the bearish trend.

## 2. Change of Character (CHOCH)
* **Bullish CHOCH**: Price closes above the last Lower High (LH) in a downtrend. Signals shift from bearish to bullish.
* **Bearish CHOCH**: Price closes below the last Higher Low (HL) in an uptrend. Signals shift from bullish to bearish.

## 3. Liquidity Sweep / Stop Hunt
* **Setup**: Price spikes past a prominent swing high/low or EQH/EQL level but fails to hold, closing back inside the range.
* **Action**: Signals exhaustion and potential reversal in the opposite direction of the sweep.

## 4. Volatility Squeeze (Compression)
* **Setup**: Average True Range (ATR) drops below the 20-period historical average, and volume compresses.
* **Action**: Indicates market is building energy for an explosive breakout. Avoid range entries; wait for expansion trigger.
